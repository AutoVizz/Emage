3. Server actions

Granular progressive keys

One key per DWT resolution level, coarse-first. Level 0 is the coarsest approximation. Hand a recipient K0..Ka and they can decode the image at that resolution and no finer — the levels above stay encrypted. These are derived in the browser and are never sent to the server.
